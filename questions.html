Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, safety, timings and party planning with Vicko's Face Painting.

How do I book a party?

Give me a call, or send your details through the enquiry form on the home page. I’ll get back to you to confirm everything over the phone and then send a quick, easy booking summary so you know exactly what to expect.

What areas do you serve?

I serve Rolleston, Lincoln, West Melton and nearby Selwyn communities. If your event is a little further away, just ask and I’ll see how I can help.

Are the paints safe for children?

Yes. I use professional, hypoallergenic face paints designed for sensitive skin, and all products are easy to remove with warm water and gentle cleanser.

How long does each face painting design take?

Most simple designs take 5–8 minutes, while more detailed looks can take up to 12 minutes. This means plenty of guests can be painted during a party without long waits.

Do you bring tables and supplies?

Yes. I bring all paints, brushes, wipes, cleaning supplies, and a comfortable painting setup.

What happens if the weather changes?

If the event is outdoors and the weather changes, I can move to a sheltered area.
